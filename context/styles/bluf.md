#### BLUF (bottom line up front, Army AR 25-50)

Put the conclusion first, so that a reader who stops after one sentence still has the point.

- The banner is the bottom line. Its first sentence states the outcome, the decision or the action the note asks for. Never open it with background.
- Open each section with its main point in one sentence, then give the support: evidence, reasons and details.
- When the note needs something from the reader (a decision, a review or an action), state that request in the banner, and say which role must act.
- Use the active voice, short sentences and short paragraphs.
- Cut background that does not support the bottom line.
