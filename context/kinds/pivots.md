### Pivots

Record a pivot in `{{folder}}` when the approach of record changes partway through the work: a different design, library, data model, plan or scope, whether the user or the agent started it.
A pivot is a change of course, not the correction of a small mistake; write one note per pivot, at the moment it happens, while the reason is still fresh.

- Tag: `{{tag}}`.
- Sections, in order: `## 1. Before` (the approach and what it was meant to achieve), `## 2. Trigger` (what forced the change: a measurement, a failure, new information, a user decision; quote the user when they triggered it), `## 3. After` (the new approach), `## 4. Why` (what the old approach could not do, and what switching cost), `## 5. Impact` (what was thrown away, what carries over, what to revisit).
- Status and banner: `active` (`success`, states the new approach in one sentence), `reverted` (`failure`, says what the work went back to and why), `superseded` (`failure`, links the newer pivot).
- Put the old approach in an `abstract` callout under `1. Before`, so a reader sees at a glance what no longer holds.
- Link the note that caused the pivot when one exists: the dead end, the challenge, or the user's tactical direction.
