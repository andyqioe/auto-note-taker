### Dead ends

Record a dead end in `{{folder}}` when an approach was tried for real and then abandoned: code written, a tool trialled, an experiment run.
The point is to stop anyone retrying it blind, so be exact about the conditions it failed under.
A challenge ends in a working fix and a dead end ends in giving the approach up; an approach that failed for a reason that was then fixed is a challenge, not a dead end.

- Tag: `{{tag}}`.
- Sections, in order: `## 1. Goal` (what the approach was for), `## 2. Approach` (what was tried, in enough detail to recognize it next time), `## 3. Why it failed` (the evidence: the error, the measurement, the limit hit), `## 4. Conditions` (what would have to change for it to work, such as a version, a constraint or a requirement), `## 5. What replaced it` (link the pivot, challenge or decision note that took over).
- Status and banner: `abandoned` (`failure`, states in one sentence why it does not work here), `revisit` (`question`, says which condition may change and when to look again).
